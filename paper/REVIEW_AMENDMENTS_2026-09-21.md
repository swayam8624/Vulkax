# Reality Probe reviewer-amendment ledger — 2026-09-21

This ledger records amendments made after review of the complete internal manuscript.
It distinguishes manuscript hardening from new scientific evidence.

## Applied

1. **Title narrowed to the demonstrated result.**  
   Changed the paper-facing title from *Counterfactual Physical Verification of
   Captured Worlds* to *Information-Limited Physical Verification of Captured Worlds*.
   This avoids implying that the paper establishes a universal successful verifier.

2. **Reality Probe made the contribution-level framework.**  
   The abstract, introduction, contribution list, and conclusion now explicitly
   distinguish Reality Probe from DCS. Reality Probe is the evidence-governed
   proposal/verification/refusal architecture. DCS is one mechanism-selective
   instrument tested inside that architecture.

3. **DCS negative result retained as science rather than hidden.**  
   The contribution list explicitly includes prospective falsification of DCS in the
   tested regimes. D2, D3, D4V, and fresh OFC zero-crossing results remain visible.

4. **Broken equation reference repaired.**  
   The explanatory second-difference equation now has
   `\\label{eq:second}`, and the prose uses `Eq.~\\ref{eq:second}` instead of the
   literal broken `Eq. [eq:second]` text.

5. **Decision-threshold sensitivity added without retuning.**  
   The frozen primary magnitude remains tau=2. A post-hoc audit records that DCS has
   zero fresh crossings even at tau=1 and force compliance has zero fresh crossings
   for all tau>=1.5. At tau=1 the aggregate ledger proves at least one force crossing,
   but an exact count is not asserted because the frozen publication ledger does not
   retain the complete signed per-proposal vector.

6. **Classical experiment-design positioning strengthened.**  
   The manuscript now directly situates the work relative to model discrimination and
   optimal experiment design and explicitly states that choosing informative
   excitation is not claimed as a new principle.

7. **Information-limit wording tightened.**  
   Wording that could be read as establishing information content as the unique causal
   limitation was softened. The result supports experiment-dependent observability;
   it does not exclude every other possible limitation.

8. **Stage-table semantics clarified.**  
   The table heading now uses “Crosses frozen boundary” rather than a potentially
   misleading bare “Resolved”, and the prose explains that zero boundary crossings do
   not mean an experiment produced no scientific information.

9. **Figure-path integrity repaired.**  
   The manuscript originally referenced a nonexistent `paper/figures/` directory.
   Existing images now resolve to committed assets under `docs/readme_assets/`.
   A missing manuscript-only architecture image was removed as a dangling dependency
   and its content preserved in prose. The overview uses the committed storyboard.

10. **Repository paper state updated.**  
    The manuscript dossier now records that `paper/main.tex` exists and that the next
    phase is submission engineering, not manuscript construction. The positioning
    source note was tightened to match the revised claim boundary.
