\subsection{Measured-source engineering
check}\label{measured-source-engineering-check}

The end-to-end system is also exercised on measured DOT C2 trajectories
(Xinyuan Li et al.~2024). The importer keeps measured 3D correspondences
separate from explicit model proxies for rest state, density, thickness,
mass, Gaussian photometry, and uncertainty. Fit-only calibration and

rather than a claim of true cloth-property recovery. A proposed local
material rewrite is rejected and rolled back because the complete
independent derivative-verification contract is not met, despite
satisfying the configured nonlinear linearization-error tolerance. This
result validates the commit/refusal mechanism on measured-source data
but is not used as evidence for the central deceptive-repair claim.

\section{Results and Analysis}\label{results-and-analysis}

\subsection{Deceptive repair is a distinct failure
mode}\label{deceptive-repair-is-a-distinct-failure-mode}

The D4V and fresh OFC populations establish that lower ordinary held-out
error does not uniquely determine whether the untouched physical target
improves. In D4V, 14 of 36 ordinary-improving repairs are deceptive. In
the fresh OFC population, 12 of 36 are deceptive. These counts are not
intended as estimates of a universal deception rate; they establish that
the failure mode occurs under controlled candidate-generation pipelines
and must therefore be considered by any verification rule that approves
physical edits from observation fit alone.

\subsection{The DCS hypothesis is falsified as a general prospective
verifier}\label{the-dcs-hypothesis-is-falsified-as-a-general-prospective-verifier}

The constructed D1 control demonstrates that the implementation can
produce a strong dark-field rejection when the experiment is engineered
to contain that distinction. The subsequent stages do not preserve that
strength. D2 and D3 remain far below the decision magnitude, DCS does
not dominate matched ranking baselines, and D4V provides zero resolved
coverage. These negative results are central rather than incidental:
they rule out the stronger interpretation that mechanism-selective
cancellation alone is sufficient for repair certification in the tested
regime.

The D3 numerical refinement result is particularly informative. Reducing
the witness-space numerical floor by several-fold does not materially
increase standardized separation. The limiting factor is therefore not
simply solver discretization error in the original witness evaluation.

\subsection{Orthogonal physical information changes the evidence
scale}\label{orthogonal-physical-information-changes-the-evidence-scale}

We interpret this as strong evidence that observability depends on the experiment, while avoiding the stronger claim that information content is the unique limiting cause.

This result supports the interpretation that verification quality
depends strongly on which physical question is asked. Kinematic
observations that are adequate for proposing a plausible repair need not
be adequate for discriminating that repair from alternatives. A
force-controlled experiment adds information that is not available to
the fitting objective.

\subsection{Stronger evidence is not sufficient
evidence}\label{stronger-evidence-is-not-sufficient-evidence}

The strongest result in the fresh force population is \(|z| = 1.31911\),
still below the decision magnitude. The selected visual example has
signed force progress \(z = - 1.31811\): evidence moves substantially in
the veto direction but remains unresolved. The system therefore refuses
certification. We view this behavior as preferable to lowering the
threshold after observing the partition or interpreting an intermediate
score as a definitive physical verdict.

The information-frontier view also avoids a misleading binary narrative.
The force channel is not equivalent to DCS simply because both return
0\% resolved coverage. Their evidence magnitudes are materially
different, and the remaining gap to the decision rule is correspondingly
different. The experiment therefore supplies a quantitative direction
for future sensor and intervention design even though it does not
support deployment-level verification.

\section{Discussion}\label{discussion}

\subsection{What is established}\label{what-is-established}

The experiments support three linked conclusions. First, observationally
improving physical repairs can be deceptive with respect to an untouched
target. Second, mechanism-sensitive verification signals can remain too
weak to support a reliable repair decision even when their numerical
implementation is stable. Third, changing to a genuinely different
physical channel can substantially increase observability without
necessarily making the evidence sufficient for certification.

These conclusions are narrower than a universal physical-correctness
claim, but they are operational. A persistent captured-world system can
preserve the distinction between fitting, verification, and unresolved
evidence, and it can refuse a requested rewrite without treating refusal
as a pipeline failure.

\subsection{Relation to identifiability and experiment
design}\label{relation-to-identifiability-and-experiment-design}

The force-compliance result is consistent with the broader principle
that parameter and model discrimination depend on excitation and
measurement design (Asadi and Laksari 2025; Y. Wang et al.~2026). Our
experiments do not introduce that principle. Their contribution is to
expose its consequence inside a physical-rewrite workflow: verification
can fail not because a candidate looks implausible, but because the
available experiment does not separate plausible candidates strongly
enough. This distinction changes the system response from ``pick the
best score'' to ``request more informative evidence or remain
unresolved.''

\subsection{Relation to simulation verification and digital
twins}\label{relation-to-simulation-verification-and-digital-twins}

Simulation credibility frameworks already separate code verification,
solution verification, model validation, and uncertainty (Oberkampf,
Pilch, and Trucano 2007). Digital-twin validation work similarly
emphasizes which counterfactual claims are supported by observable
validation data (Laudy 2026). Reality Probe should therefore be read as
an application-specific mechanism rather than a replacement for those
frameworks. Its scope is a physically editable captured world where a
candidate rewrite is proposed and an independent intervention is used to
decide whether the rewrite should be committed.

\subsection{Limitations}\label{limitations}

The main limitations are substantive.

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Fresh prospective force evidence is synthetic.} The orthogonal
  force-compliance experiment uses controlled MPM truth worlds rather
  than a measured force-sensor setup. A real prospective force
  experiment is required for a stronger measured-world claim.
\item
  \textbf{GAUGE is retrospective.} The measured channel contradiction
  motivates the problem and demonstrates that different observables can
  disagree, but it is not an untouched prospective test of DCS or force
  compliance.
\item
  \textbf{The physical regime is limited.} The principal experiments use
  a fixed deformable MPM setup and selected APIC/PIC candidate families.
  Generalization across constitutive families, contact regimes, and
  solvers is not established.
\item
  \textbf{The decision magnitude is operational.} The value 2 is frozen before evaluation and prevents post-hoc threshold tuning, but it is not a universal law for all physical verification tasks. A post-hoc robustness audit shows zero fresh crossings for both channels at every $\tau\ge1.5$; the exact force crossing count at $\tau=1$ cannot be recovered from the aggregate publication ledger alone and is therefore not asserted.
\item
  \textbf{DCS synthesis is heuristic.} Lower-moment annihilation is
  exact up to tolerance, but the projected maximin search is not
  globally optimal.
\item
  \textbf{The response-jet interpretation is operational.} The
  implementation estimates order of contact from validated witnesses; it
  does not reconstruct a complete symbolic Taylor tensor.
\item
  \textbf{The selected hero case is post hoc.} It is chosen
  deterministically for explanation and does not influence thresholds,
  aggregate statistics, or the information-gain result.
\item
  \textbf{Spatial residuals are explanatory.} Solver-driven residual
  fields localize response disagreement but are not themselves the DCS
  decision statistic.
\item
  \textbf{Measured DOT parameters are model-conditioned.} The DOT
  benchmark lacks stress-free state, loads, thickness, mass, and
  constitutive ground truth for C2; fitted material values are therefore
  not presented as true cloth properties.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future work}\label{future-work}

The most direct next step is not another retuning of the frozen DCS or
force partitions. It is a separately frozen measured experiment with an
independent force sensor or another calibrated excitation that
materially changes the available physical information. Modal or
frequency-domain excitation, richer constitutive families, and
intervention-specific sensor design are plausible directions. Any such
experiment should keep proposal generation blind to the confirmatory
channel and should preserve a predeclared support/veto/unresolved rule.

\section{Reproducibility and Implementation
Notes}\label{reproducibility-and-implementation-notes}

The repository records the scientific freeze dated 2026-09-21 at commit
a9da8c0aa868. The one-command runner builds and tests the code, executes
the staged experiments, regenerates deterministic figures and tables,
validates frozen numerical values, captures compiler/system/GPU
provenance, and assembles a SHA-256-indexed evidence archive. Scientific
conclusions are read from machine-readable JSON ledgers rather than from
rounded figure labels.

The core DCS implementation is separated from experiment drivers and
includes arbitrary-order lower-moment validation, signed response
application, finite-amplitude counterfactual cumulants, standardized
discrepancies, uncertainty propagation, active stencil synthesis,
witness-space numerical refinement, and spatial localization. The
orthogonal force protocol and fresh truth worlds are versioned
separately from the earlier D2/D3/D4V partitions. This separation is
used to prevent a negative gate from being silently replaced by a
retuned result on the same data.

Paper-facing visualizations are deterministic. Solver-driven surfaces
may use an explicit display magnification to make micrometer-scale
motion visible; reported numerical values are unscaled. The Stanford
Bunny, where used in presentation assets, is a visualization carrier
rather than benchmark geometry and requires source attribution. The
companion mathematical explainer uses the actual solver body and does
not require the bunny.

\section{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

A physically editable captured world should not equate better
observation fit with better physics. Across controlled repair
populations, we find ordinary-improving repairs that worsen an untouched
physical target. Reality Probe provides the evidence-governed transaction around such repairs; DCS is one tested mechanism-selective instrument inside it. Frozen D2, D3, and D4V experiments do not support a claim that DCS is a general prospective verifier. A separately
frozen known-force compliance channel increases median standardized
evidence by 11.46x on fresh truth worlds, yet still leaves every
proposal below the decision magnitude. The resulting lesson is
practical: physical verification is constrained by the information
content of the experiment, and a trustworthy system should explicitly
refuse certification when that information is insufficient.
