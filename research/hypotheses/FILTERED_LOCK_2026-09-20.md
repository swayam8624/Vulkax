# Vulkax hypothesis filter lock — 2026-09-20

This file closes the broad discovery phase. The original combinatorial matrix contains
360 search candidates. They were never intended to become 360 independent paper
claims; they cross representations, failure sources, evidence interventions,
diagnostics and decisions. The filter below records which *claim families* survived
falsification and novelty attack.

## Locked filtering rule

A family survives only if it satisfies all of:

1. it is not already the obvious headline of 2024-2026 inverse-physics / Gaussian /
   physical-world-model work;
2. it cannot be reduced to generic identifiability, uncertainty quantification,
   selective prediction, simulator validation, active model discrimination, or
   optimal experiment design;
3. Vulkax already has at least one executable positive control or falsification
   mechanism for it;
4. negative evidence is preserved rather than threshold-tuned away;
5. it admits a measured-data test and an independent target intervention;
6. it yields an operational system action, not only a diagnostic score.

## Surviving evidence-backed observations

1. **Held-out replay is not counterfactual support.** Wrong physics can fit observed
   motion yet fail under an unseen intervention.
2. **Evidence insufficiency can be repairable.** Sensitivity-selected additional
   interventions repaired synthetic fine/coarse APIC cases while wrong
   model/transfer families remained bad.
3. **A single risk score is not enough.** Validation-1 degraded and Validation-2
   collapsed to zero coverage.
4. **Numerical inadequacy is a distinct refusal cause.** Deep timestep refinement
   eventually reached an asymptotic-like regime, while spatial/transfer refinement
   remained unresolved.
5. **Measured structural mismatch can be diagnosed without fitting material
   parameters.** GAUGE no-fit APIC initially lost to a zero-parameter affine null
   on all repeats.
6. **A measured-only support hypothesis can survive disjoint evaluation.** The
   GAUGE effective deforming span was stable across thresholds/materials/repeats,
   and the odd-repeat-calibrated finite-support pilot improved both face and marker
   errors on all ten even-repeat evaluations.
7. **Weak observation evidence should be represented as a scientific refusal, not
   a software failure.**

## The one surviving primary family

### Intervention-conditioned adequacy routing for physical rewrites

The surviving question is not “is this scene physically accurate?” in the abstract.

It is:

> For a *specific requested physical edit/intervention*, can a captured-world
> system determine which adequacy layer prevents the requested counterfactual from
> being trusted, route to a repair that is appropriate for that failure cause, and
> independently re-verify the repaired world before committing the edit?

The required failure causes are at least:

- observation/evidence insufficiency;
- observation-to-physical-support / boundary mismatch;
- numerical discretization or transfer inadequacy;
- model-family / constitutive inadequacy;
- parameter evidence / identifiability insufficiency;
- correspondence / representation support mismatch.

This is the only primary family authorized for continued Vulkax paper-directed
implementation.

## What would kill the locked family

The family must be killed or demoted if any of the following occurs:

1. a direct prior-art system is found that already performs requested-edit-specific
   multi-cause adequacy attribution, cause-specific repair routing, and independent
   post-repair counterfactual re-verification;
2. reason-coded routing does not beat scalar UQ / held-out-residual / always-refuse
   baselines on risk-coverage and repair success;
3. cause attribution is not stable under fresh synthetic domains with known truth;
4. the real GAUGE structural-repair case does not survive the definitive
   high-resolution held-out gate;
5. targeted evidence requests do not outperform generic Fisher-information /
   max-motion / random interventions for *target-counterfactual risk reduction*;
6. the method succeeds only on one simulator-specific pathology and does not
   transfer across at least two failure families and two benchmark regimes.

## Publication posture

No venue acceptance is guaranteed. The research target is Tier-1 quality only if
the method becomes an algorithmic contribution with cross-cause, cross-domain,
independent validation. A paper consisting only of the current diagnostics should
not be submitted as the flagship Vulkax paper.


## Post-DCS execution update

The historical conclusion above predates the complete DCS implementation/elimination
sequence and is retained for provenance.

Subsequent evidence changed the state:

1. CAR-v1 / generic adequacy routing was superseded by DCS after the definitive
   GAUGE finite-support metric mirage.
2. DCS fixed order-2 ranking failed fresh validation.
3. DCS witness-space uncertainty and adaptive k=2/3 failed to produce resolved
   prospective coverage.
4. Pair-specific DCS repair veto also failed its frozen discovery advancement gate.
5. GAUGE retrospectively demonstrates that mechanism-specific longitudinal
   dark-field evidence can contradict aggregate marker/face evidence, but this case
   cannot validate prospective generalization.

Therefore **no family is currently authorized as a positive locked flagship**.

The broad discovery/filtering phase is still closed in the sense that the generic
families listed above remain killed. The next search is constrained to a narrower
question:

> What new physical measurement or intervention channel can amplify
> mechanism-relevant disagreement enough to survive measurement + numerical
> uncertainty, while retaining an independent target counterfactual and a fresh
> untouched validation partition?

Frozen D2/D3/D4V partitions may not be reused for method tuning.
