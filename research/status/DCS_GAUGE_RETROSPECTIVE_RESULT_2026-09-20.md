## Interpretation

The result is channel-specific:

- marker-space dark-field agrees with the ordinary observation metric and prefers
  the finite-support repair;
- longitudinal mechanism dark-field rejects that repair in **9/10** repeats.

This is useful evidence for the original Vulkax failure mode:

> an observationally better repair can remain mechanically worse in a
> mechanism-specific residual after lower-order response is removed.

However, the case was already known before DCS was designed. It is therefore a
retrospective diagnostic demonstration, **not** evidence of prospective
generalization.

The implementation must preserve both channels rather than collapse them into a
single favorable score.
