## Strongest research family now

The strongest surviving family is a compound question:

> Given a persistent captured physical world, can the system determine whether its evidence is sufficient for a requested intervention, quantify how far the counterfactual can be trusted relative to model/numerical uncertainty, refuse unsafe edits for the correct reason, and identify the next measurement/intervention that would make the edit verifiable?

That is not yet a novelty claim. It is the current highest-value target to attack.
