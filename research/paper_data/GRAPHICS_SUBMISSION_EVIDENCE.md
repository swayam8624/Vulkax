# Reality Probe graphics-submission evidence matrix

This pass is deliberately venue-facing rather than a new scientific phase. The frozen research result remains unchanged. The purpose is to make the evidence legible to a graphics reviewer and to prevent the manuscript from reading like a verification paper with graphics merely attached.

## Benchmark datasets

| Source | Type | Role in paper | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DOT C2 | Public measured trajectory data | End-to-end captured-world engineering check | Measured-source import, calibration/replay, rewrite refusal, provenance, rollback | Prospective validation of DCS or a material-identification claim |
| GAUGE foam shearing | Public measured physical data | Retrospective measured contradiction | Demonstrates that ordinary marker/face preference and a longitudinal mechanism channel can disagree on real data | Fresh prospective confirmation; the conflict was known before DCS was designed |
| D1/D2/D3/D4V | Frozen synthetic benchmark families | Controlled method evaluation | Positive control, frozen validation, witness-space study, repair-veto population | Universal real-world deception rates |
| Orthogonal force compliance | Fresh synthetic follow-on | Orthogonal-information experiment | Shows an 11.455x increase in median standardized evidence without crossing the frozen decision threshold | A validated prospective verifier |

## Visual-quality / visual-evidence package

## Submission rule

Every visual claim must trace to frozen solver output or a clearly marked explanatory graphic. Display magnification is permitted for legibility only when the caption states the factor and the reported numerical values remain unscaled.

The graphics-facing paper should therefore present a three-layer evidence stack:

1. public measured-source benchmarks (DOT C2, GAUGE),
2. frozen quantitative populations and matched baselines,
3. deterministic visual examples that expose the spatial/physical consequence of those quantitative results.

The scientific freeze is not reopened by this pass.
