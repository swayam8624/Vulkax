# Vulkax Paper Positioning — Frozen Research Story

Date: 2026-09-21

This is a research-positioning source note, not manuscript prose.

## Central question

When a physical edit to a captured executable world improves ordinary held-out
observations, does that mean the edited world is physically better — and can an
independent verifier tell the difference?

## Primary contribution structure

### C1 — Deceptive physical repair

Formalize the failure mode:

```math
L_{obs}(R_1) < L_{obs}(R_0)
```

while:

```math
L_{mechanism}(R_1) > L_{mechanism}(R_0).
```

The key claim is not that every improving repair is deceptive. It is that ordinary
held-out improvement is insufficient evidence of physical correctness.

### C2 — Evidence-governed counterfactual verification laboratory

Vulkax implements:

- signed intervention contrasts;
- exact lower-moment annihilation;
- Möbius finite-amplitude interaction terms;
- uncertainty-aware separation;
- active stencil synthesis;
- witness-space numerical refinement;
- support / veto / unresolved decisions;
- transactional commit / refusal / rollback.

DCS is presented as the principal mechanism-selective verifier investigated by the
paper, not as a universally successful method.

### C3 — Deceptive-repair benchmark and falsification sequence

Frozen D4V population:

- 36 ordinary-heldout-improving repair proposals;
- 14 deceptive;
- 22 beneficial;
- DCS resolved coverage: 0%;
- matched raw/Fisher/max-motion verification coverage: 0%.

This establishes both the existence of the repair failure mode and the inadequacy of
the tested verification information regime.

### C4 — Quantified information frontier

D4V median DCS |z| is about 0.050 against a frozen resolved-decision reference of 2.

The result is not merely “method failed.” The paper quantifies the missing
signal-to-uncertainty margin.

### C5 — Orthogonal-information intervention

A separately preregistered known-force compliance experiment uses:

- new truth worlds;
- a force-controlled physical channel not used to fit/propose repairs;
- the same |z| >= 2 decision rule.

Fresh result:

- DCS median |z|: 0.04877;
- force median |z|: 0.55863;
- median information gain: 11.455×;
- force best |z|: 1.31911;
- force resolved coverage: 0%.

This is causal evidence for the information-content interpretation: changing the
physical channel dramatically increases observability, but the tested channel still
does not reach credible decision coverage.

### C6 — Measured retrospective contradiction

GAUGE:

- aggregate ordinary metrics favor overlap 10/10;
- marker dark-field favors overlap 10/10;
- longitudinal mechanism dark-field favors endpoint 9/10.

This is retrospective and must remain labelled as such.

## Final scientific thesis

The paper should defend:

> Observational improvement is not equivalent to physical improvement in captured
> executable worlds. Mechanism-sensitive verification can itself fail because the
> experiment does not contain enough discriminating physical information. Adding an
> orthogonal physical channel can sharply increase observability without necessarily
> reaching a credible verification decision.

## What the paper is not

It is not:

- a claim that DCS is a solved verifier;
- a claim that the force channel is deployable;
- a new finite-difference theorem;
- a new Möbius inversion theorem;
- a new optimal-experiment-design theorem;
- a measured force-sensor validation paper.

## Why unresolved decisions are publishable here

“Unresolved” is the system-level decision.

The research-level result is resolved:

- the frozen channels did not clear the credibility threshold;
- the amount of missing information is quantified;
- a new orthogonal channel increases the signal by 11.46×;
- that gain is still insufficient.

Thus unresolved support/veto outputs are evidence for the information-limit claim,
not missing execution.

## Stop rule

Do not run additional same-partition DCS/force variants for this paper.

A new physical sensor or excitation design requires:

1. a separately frozen protocol;
2. new untouched truth/measurement data;
3. a new claim boundary.

Otherwise manuscript work begins from the current frozen evidence.
